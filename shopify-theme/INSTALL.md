## 🚀 Installation Steps

### 1. Access Your Shopify Admin
- Go to your Shopify store admin panel
- Navigate to **Online Store** → **Themes**

### 2. Upload the Theme
- Click the **Add theme** button (top right)
- Select **Upload ZIP file**
- Choose `b-just-be-theme.zip` from your Downloads or the location above
- Wait for the upload to complete (should take 10-30 seconds)

### 3. Preview Before Publishing
- Once uploaded, you'll see "b. Just Be" in your theme library
- Click **Customize** to preview and edit
- Or click **Preview** to see it live without publishing

### 4. Customize Your Store
In the theme customizer, you can:
- Upload your logo (or keep the "b." text logo)
- Add hero images
- Configure navigation menus
- Set up featured collections
- Adjust colors (though they already match your app!)
- Add content sections

### 5. Publish When Ready
- Click **Publish** to make it your live theme
- Your store will now match your web app's aesthetic!
