# Shopify Collection Banner Image Prompts

Use these prompts with tools like **Midjourney**, **DALL-E 3**, or **Adobe Firefly** to generate high-quality 16:9 banner images.

## 1. Just Comfort
**Concept**: Softness, ease, removal of friction.
**Prompt**:
> "Cinematic wide shot, 16:9 aspect ratio. A high-style fashion editorial photograph of soft, textured beige linen or cotton fabric flowing gently in warm, golden hour sunlight. The image should evoke a sense of deep relaxation and ease. Earthy neutral tones (sand, charcoal, off-white). Minimalist composition with negative space on the right side for text. Ultra-detailed texture, soft focus background."

## 2. Just Quality
**Concept**: Durability, craftsmanship, things made to last.
**Prompt**:
> "Cinematic wide shot, 16:9 aspect ratio. A macro fashion photography shot of a durable denim or heavy canvas fabric seam. Focus sharply on the high-quality stitching and construction details to symbolize strength and longevity. Darker mood with premium, dramatic lighting. Minimalist aesthetic. The texture of the fabric should be tangible. Negative space available for text overlay."

## 3. Just Human
**Concept**: Connection, imperfection, warmth.
**Prompt**:
> "Cinematic wide shot, 16:9 aspect ratio. A candid, panoramic lifestyle photograph showing a genuine moment of human connection. A group of diverse people laughing or embracing, captured with slight motion blur to convey energy and life. Warm, nostalgic film grain aesthetic. Natural lighting. Imperfect but beautiful and authentic. Not staged. Editorial fashion style."
